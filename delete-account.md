# Delete your Kangaroo Jump account

You only have an account if you chose "Save my progress" and signed in with Google or Apple.

**In the app (fastest):** open Settings, tap "Delete my account" and confirm. Your account and your saved progress are deleted from our server right away. The progress on your phone stays until you uninstall the game.

**Without the app:** email matanya.asis288@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Kangaroo Jump account", from the email address you used to sign in (or, if you hid your email with Apple, tell us so and we'll help you confirm it's you). We delete the account and its saved progress within 30 days and reply when it's done.

**What is deleted:** your account number, email address, saved progress and racer name. Nothing is kept afterwards. Purchases stay with Google Play or the App Store under your store account.
