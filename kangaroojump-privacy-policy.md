# Kangaroo Jump: Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Kangaroo Jump is a game for Wear OS watches, made by As Is. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

## What the app uses

- **Motion sensors.** The game reads the watch's accelerometer and gravity sensor so you can play by moving your arm. These readings are processed on the watch, in real time, and are never stored or sent anywhere.
- **Your game progress.** Levels you've finished, scores, coins, stars and settings are saved on your watch. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may include this small file in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by us.

## What the app does not do

- No account and no sign-in.
- No personal information is collected, stored or shared.
- No analytics, no tracking and no ads.
- No internet connection is used by the game itself.

## Purchases

The optional one-time "Unlock all levels" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google's processing of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
