# Kangaroo Jump: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Kangaroo Jump is a game for Wear OS watches, Android phones and tablets, and iPhone and iPad, made by Matanya Asis, trading as As Is ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the information described here. Contact: matanya.asis288@gmail.com. This policy explains what each version does with your information.

The short version: the **watch app** collects nothing. The **phone and tablet apps** (Android, iPhone and iPad) are free and show ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads. If you race friends online, your race name and moves pass through our game server while the race runs and are not kept. We don't keep any other personal information.

## All versions

- **Your game progress.** Levels you've finished, scores, coins, boomerangs and settings are saved on your device. If device backup is on (Android backup with your Google account, or iCloud backup on iPhone and iPad), it may include this small file. That backup is handled by Google or Apple under your account, not by us.
- **No account and no sign-in.** We don't ask for your email or any other personal details. The only things you can type in are an optional display name for challenges and a race name for online races (see below).
- **Purchases.** The optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase in the phone and tablet apps is handled entirely by Google Play, or on iPhone and iPad by the App Store. (The watch app is free, with every level open as you play; an earlier watch purchase still counts as Remove ads on the phone.) We never see your payment details. Your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) or Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Watch app

- **Motion sensors.** The game reads the watch's accelerometer and gravity sensor so you can play by moving your arm. These readings are processed on the watch, in real time, and are never stored or sent anywhere.
- The watch app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Phone and tablet apps: online races with friends

You can race friends online from "Race a friend". Online races use Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), a hosting service we use only to pass race messages between the phones in a room.

- **What is sent while you are in a room:** the room's four-letter code, a random ID made fresh each time the app starts (not linked to you or your device), your race name (one you type, or one the game picks for you), the kangaroo costume you race in, and your moves during the race (position, items used, finishing place). Your device's IP address is seen by the server to make the connection, as with any internet service.
- **Who sees it:** the other players in the same room see your race name, costume and moves. Players under 13 race under a name the game picks (see Children). Anyone with the room's code or invite link can join that room, so share codes only with people you know, and don't use your real full name as a race name.
- **How long it is kept:** race messages are passed on live and kept only in the server's memory while the room is open. We don't store them in a database, and we don't use them for ads or profiles. Supabase may keep short-lived technical logs (such as connection records) for security, as described at https://supabase.com/privacy.
- Online races are optional; the adventure, the Daily Run and races against the Roobots work without them.

## Phone and tablet apps: sharing and challenges

- **Share cards.** When you tap "Share" (after a Daily Run, a finished world, Boss Maggie, three gold boomerangs or a new kangaroo), the app draws a picture of the moment on your device and opens the system share sheet. Nothing is sent until you pick an app to share with, and then only to that app. We never receive it.
- **Challenge links ("Beat my Daily Run").** A challenge link contains the day of the Daily Run, your score and, only if you type one, a display name. The name is optional, kept only on your phone (so you don't have to type it again) and only sent inside the links you choose to share. The challenge details are placed after the "#" in the link, which browsers do not send to the web server.
- **Opening a challenge.** If the game is installed, the link opens it directly. If not, the link's web page (hosted on GitHub Pages at matanyaasis288.github.io, a static page with no cookies, analytics or ads) sends you to Google Play or the App Store. GitHub may keep technical logs of visits as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
- **After installing from a challenge.** On Android, the Play Store link carries the challenge (day, score and name, if any) as the install "referrer", and the game reads it once on its first launch through Google Play's Install Referrer service so the challenge can open. This happens between Google Play and the app on your phone; we don't receive it.
- Challenges never unlock anything and are not linked to purchases.

## Android phone and tablet app only: camera selfies

The Android phone and tablet app lets you take a selfie with the game's quokka (from the Quokka selfies album, or after meeting a quokka in a level). The iPhone and iPad app and the watch app have no camera feature and never use the camera.

- **Camera permission.** The app asks to use the camera only when you open the selfie camera, and explains why first. You can say no and keep playing; the rest of the game doesn't need it. You can turn it off again at any time in your phone's settings.
- **Everything happens on your phone.** The live picture, the filters (Golden Hour, Beach Day, Night Glow), the cartoon characters and the frame are all drawn on your device. No photo, video or camera image is uploaded by the game, and we never receive any.
- **Placing the quokka next to your face.** If "By my face" is on, the app uses Android's built-in on-device face detector to find where a face is in the live picture, only to put the quokka beside it. It only works out a position and size on the screen, in memory, while the camera is open. No face data, face template or biometric information is stored, sent or used to identify anyone, and no extra software or model is downloaded for it.
- **Nothing is kept unless you choose.** A photo you take is held in memory until you leave the screen. It's saved only if you tap "Save" (into the app's private album on your phone, which you can delete from the album at any time and which is left out of the app's device backup) or "Save to gallery" (into your phone's Pictures/Kangaroo Jump folder, managed by you like any other photo). Uninstalling the app deletes its private album.
- **Sharing is up to you.** "Share" opens the system share sheet; the picture goes only to the app you pick, under that app's own policy. If the "Instagram Stories" button is shown, it opens Instagram's own story editor with the picture; nothing is posted until you post it there.

## Phone and tablet apps: ads

The phone and tablet versions are free and show ads from Google AdMob: a banner under the level map, a full-screen ad between some levels, and optional video ads you can choose to watch to continue a level.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's AdMob service may collect from your phone (for players under 13, see Children below for the limits on this):

- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
- information about the ads you see and how you interact with them,
- diagnostic and performance information about the ad service.

Google handles this information as described in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We don't receive this information and can't link it to you.

**Your choices**

- Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy settings" in the app.
- On iPhone and iPad, the app asks for permission to track (Apple's App Tracking Transparency) before any personalized ads; if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you only see non-personalized ads. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Players under 13 are never asked (see Children).
- On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your phone).
- The one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns off the banners and the ads between levels.

## Children

Kangaroo Jump is made for everyone, children included. On phones and tablets (Android, iPhone and iPad) the game asks once, on first launch, "What year were you born?". The answer is never sent anywhere: the app keeps only whether you are 13 or over, on your device, and asks again a year later for anyone under 13.

For players under 13 (and for anyone who hasn't answered yet):

- **Ads are child-directed.** Every ad request is marked as directed to a child and under the age of consent, so Google AdMob serves only non-personalized ads rated for general audiences (G), and doesn't use the device to build an advertising profile, as described in Google's policies for child-directed requests (https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431). On iPhone and iPad they are never asked for permission to track. On Android phones and tablets, a child's full-screen ads between levels are image ads only (no video), shown only after a level they won and at most once every five minutes, and a child is never offered an ad to continue a level.
- **No typed names online.** In online races, a child races under a name the game picks; they can't type a name of their own, so nothing a child writes is shared with other players.
- **Selfies stay on the device**, as for everyone (see above), and no face data is ever stored. A child can save a selfie, but the game shows no "Share" or "Instagram Stories" button for it, and no link to the game's Instagram page.
- **Nothing is shared from the game.** On Android phones and tablets, a child gets no "Share" or "Challenge a friend" buttons (score cards, new kangaroos, quokka postcards), so no picture or typed name leaves the game from a child.
- We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and we don't ask for any.

Parents and guardians: if you have a question, or think your child's information was shared, email matanya.asis288@gmail.com and we'll help.

## How long information is kept

Apart from race messages, which are kept only while a room is open (see above), we don't collect or keep any of your personal information. Your game progress, settings and any saved selfies stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Information Google collects through ads is kept as described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

## Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We pass race messages because you asked to race (to provide the feature you requested). Personalized ads are shown only with your consent, which you can change at any time from "Privacy settings"; otherwise ads are based on Google's legitimate interests and limited data as Google describes. Supabase and Google may process information outside your country, under their own data transfer safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

## Your rights

Because we don't keep personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can still contact us about any privacy question, and if you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland you can also complain to your local data protection authority. For information Google collects through ads, you can use Google's controls at https://myadcenter.google.com and the rights described in the Google Privacy Policy.
