# Kangaroo Jump: Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Kangaroo Jump is a game for Wear OS watches and Android phones, made by As Is. This policy explains what each version does with your information.

The short version: the **watch app** collects nothing. The **phone app** is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads. We (As Is) never receive or store your personal information.

## Both apps

- **Your game progress.** Levels you've finished, scores, coins, stars and settings are saved on your device. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may include this small file in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by us.
- **No account and no sign-in.** We don't ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
- **Purchases.** The optional one-time purchase ("Unlock all levels" on the watch, "Remove ads" on the phone; buying it on either unlocks both) is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google's processing of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Watch app

- **Motion sensors.** The game reads the watch's accelerometer and gravity sensor so you can play by moving your arm. These readings are processed on the watch, in real time, and are never stored or sent anywhere.
- The watch app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Phone app: ads

The phone version is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the menus, a full-screen ad between some levels, and optional video ads you can choose to watch to continue a level.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's AdMob service may collect from your phone:

- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
- information about the ads you see and how you interact with them,
- diagnostic and performance information about the ad service.

Google handles this information as described in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We don't receive this information and can't link it to you.

**Your choices**

- Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy settings" on the phone app's home screen.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your phone).
- The one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns off the banners and the ads between levels.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone. If you believe a child under 13 is using the phone app, you can turn off ad personalization on the device as described above or contact us.

## Your rights

Because we don't hold any personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For information Google collects through ads, you can use Google's controls at https://myadcenter.google.com and the rights described in the Google Privacy Policy.
