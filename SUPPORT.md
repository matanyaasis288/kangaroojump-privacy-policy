# Kangaroo Jump: Outback Run, support

Need help with Kangaroo Jump? Email **matanya.asis288@gmail.com** and say which device you play on (iPhone, iPad, Android phone or Wear OS watch). We usually reply within a few days.

## Common questions

**How do I play?**
Tap to jump and tap again in the air to double jump. Swipe up to open the umbrella, swipe down to slide. Settings → How to play shows every move with a short demo.

**I bought "Remove ads". How do I get it back on a new device?**
On iPhone or iPad: Settings → Restore Purchases. On Android: open the game signed in to the same Google account, and the purchase comes back by itself.

**Does "Remove ads" unlock levels?**
No. It only removes the ads. Levels open one at a time as you play.

**Is my progress saved?**
Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
