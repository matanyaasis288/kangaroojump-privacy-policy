# Kangaroo Jump: Outback Run, support

Need help with Kangaroo Jump? Email **matanya.asis288@gmail.com** and say which device you play on (iPhone, iPad, Android phone or Wear OS watch). We usually reply within a few days.

## Common questions

**How do I play?**
Tap to jump and tap again in the air to double jump. Hold to open the umbrella and glide. New moves, like the shield, are taught in the level where they first appear, and Settings → How to play shows every move with a short demo.

**I bought "Remove ads". How do I get it back on a new device?**
On iPhone or iPad: Settings → Restore Purchases. On Android: open the game signed in to the same Google account, and the purchase comes back by itself.

**Does "Remove ads" unlock levels?**
No. It only removes the ads. Levels open one at a time as you play.

**Is my progress saved?**
Progress is saved on your device, and deleting the app deletes that copy. If you're signed in to iCloud on iPhone or iPad, or to Google Play Games on Android, a copy is also kept in your own account, so your progress can come back when you reinstall or follow you to another phone or tablet.

**I saw an ad that seems wrong. How do I report it?**
Email **matanya.asis288@gmail.com** with a screenshot of the ad (or what it showed) and roughly when you saw it, and we'll block it.
